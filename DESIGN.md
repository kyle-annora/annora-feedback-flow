# Feedback Flow · original artifact iteration
This iteration applies the approved `winquote-feedback-visuals-v2` direction to the original Annora artifact. It preserves the existing record, task, history, player and state structure. It does not replace the application with a new homepage or a gallery of standalone mockups.

## One feedback process, different sample tickets

PDF, compartment and refrigeration are sample inputs to one shared customer workspace. Keep sample selection in the floating Walkthrough console, while My feedback and Updates remain the customer navigation. The original business page provides the problem context; the proposed result appears later as evidence for version-specific acceptance.

The initial team task leads with the original complaint, source-labeled facts and recorded decisions. AI organization is simulated and provisional. The team chooses whether the evidence is sufficient to draft a plan or whether a customer question is genuinely needed. New requests start with no complete scope or acceptance checklist. The explicit draft-plan action prepares a private editable draft; it neither shares the plan nor sends a question. Existing manually edited drafts and historical versions remain intact.

Express that first decision as **Use existing evidence** and **Confirm with the customer**. The first choice prepares a private plan; the second opens an editable private question draft. For the refrigeration example, make the optional equipment-return question available here before any plan is shared. Keep the historical policy as evidence, not customer consent. Manual question creation remains available. A later review treats an already answered preference as a record unless the team deliberately reopens it. Preview and Send remain distinct steps.

Use actual sent order for customer-facing question rounds, independently of internal initial/detailed categories or older saved IDs. Waiting copy must distinguish a first plan from a revision to an already shared plan. These are targeted refinements to the existing task hierarchy and typed question editor, not new navigation or a new visual direction.

In Details, Question rounds has one disclosure only. Opening it reveals static Round N sections with answer counts, saved references, questions, answers and timestamps. Separate rounds with a thin divider; do not nest another accordion or add an inner chevron. This applies equally to current and historical version context.

Apply that single-disclosure rule across the inspector. A plan or basis group has one heading, and adjacent facts with exactly the same source and status share one provenance line. Distinct source identities, content, status and ordering remain intact; source context is inline rather than another disclosure. Original feedback owns its location and historical-source qualifier. Activity keeps every event, with unique supplementary detail inline. Version navigation shows one current status plus earlier approval milestones, and explicitly distinguishes current progress from the selected version's saved content.

Historical question rounds, notes, timestamps and basis always come from that version's snapshot. If a saved candidate no longer matches a supported authored interpretation, retain its actual candidate and checks and state that the answer-to-version relationship cannot be inferred; never substitute a current catalog policy. Exact duplicate check labels/values are displayed once without altering the record.

Lead the submitted-version inspector with the answer used, its impact and the saved acceptance check. This compact summary is always visible and names the version; historical views mark it Read-only. Keep the full saved plan folded below it so the remaining context stays reachable on a phone. The adopted question is marked Used in Vn inside the unchanged chronological question history. Do not infer a relationship for unsupported older candidates or invent missing checks; show the saved answer and an explicit review-needed state instead.

When a requester asks for a change to a submitted version, the Website team can choose Ask a follow-up directly beside the adjustment. This opens a private question draft linked to that actual adjustment, starts a new scope cycle, and preserves prior answers and submitted snapshots. Preview and Send remain separate; reopening does not notify the customer or manufacture a requester correction. A revised-scope answer is labelled Qn · Scope revision n even when its internal question category is initial.

After a customer reply, lead with that actual reply and its proposed implications for behavior and acceptance. Confirming and sharing remains a separate team action. A known historical answer can legitimately eliminate a clarification; do not ask again to make the demo appear intelligent. Unsupported answers or materials stay visible for human review. Submitted versions retain their own sources and answers.

Apply the existing Cycle task/context layout, Attio's focused current decision and collapsed history, and the established Intercom teammate-screening pattern. Use compact evidence rows and a small source disclosure; avoid repeating the full approach in the customer message, editor and inspector. Preserve the approved serif/sans roles, neutral reading text and navy action color. The visible process should connect report → evidence → team judgment → optional answer → candidate/checks → exact-version acceptance → separate release approval.

Primary composition lock: [Cycle’s light feedback document](https://refero.design/pages/2b8f8897-0054-472f-8cf3-eb5b57582e87). Cycle owns the current-task column and supporting history pane. The previously researched Attio direction owns the compact, resumable walkthrough and precise controls. The user-approved serif/sans role split governs typography: Source Serif Pro for page/task/section headings, Inter for body, fields, navigation and controls. No new visual research direction supersedes that lock.

The approved V2 palette replaces the earlier indigo/lavender reading treatment. Text and broad surfaces are neutral; navy belongs to primary buttons, selected controls and small status marks. Existing green/rust evidence marks remain small and semantic. Font files are copied from the approved candidate; their licenses and original provenance are in `assets/`.

## Overview

A record opens to its current task. Past feedback, answers and delivered versions live in the right-hand Details tab, with the event timeline in Activity. After release, the main workspace contains a compact result. Details holds the released version, target and approvals. Walkthroughs start from navigation.

## Colors

In the unified customer workspace, inspector categories are 12px Inter UI labels, subordinate to the main task; they are not editorial section headings. Earlier legacy context-heading sizes no longer govern that workspace. Body copy is 14px; supporting context is 12–13px. Mobile editable fields use 16px. IDs and version tags remain monospace; measurements use tabular numerals. Do not style isolated words in a different face or accent color.

## Components

- **Feedback source.** The field grows from 56px to 320px. Its minimum rises to 76px on small screens. Focus changes its background to white and adds a 2px blue-gray outline.
